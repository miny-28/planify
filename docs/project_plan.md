                                            smart study planner

1.project overview :
smart study planner with  functional requirements :
profile for users
subject management
exam management

task management
smart planning
dynamic rescheduling
study sessions
progress & statistics
notifications & reminders
data management

2.problem statement :
students struggle to manage multiple subjects, tasks, exam, and deadline while effectively using their limited study time .
the app solves this by automatically creating and adjusting a personalizes study plan based on their priorities, deadline , progress , and available time .

3.objectives :
we had a good plan for work and study

4.target users :
students of all educational levels who need to manage subject , assignment , exams .
employees and professionals who need to organize tasks and manage deadlines .

5.non functional requirements :
the application should be easy to use
the application should respond quickly to user actions.
user data should be stored reliably
the system should maintain data consistency

 6.out of scope :
direct communication between users
teacher or manager management systems
full enterprise project management 
academic or professional advice
